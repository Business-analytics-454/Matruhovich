Project 9

Dependencies

The operation, reach, and commercial viability of casino advertising in Belarus rely on five core dependencies.

DE-1

Regulatory & licensing infrastructure. Legal advertising depends on the operator holding an active gambling licence issued by the Ministry of Taxes and Duties (MNS). Since 22 January 2026, Google accepts online gambling ads in Belarus only from operators licensed by the MNS that have also passed Google's own gambling ads certification. Outdoor placements require a permit from the local executive authority.

DE-2

Legislative stability (Law No. 225-Z "On Advertising" of 10 May 2007). Marketing budgets, creative strategies, and media-buying contracts depend on the rules of Law No. 225-Z, in particular Article 23. If MART's 2026 draft amendments (a ban on online gambling ads, a ban on mentioning betting bonuses, etc.) are adopted, existing digital acquisition funnels will stop being lawful.

DE-3

Payment & banking integration. Ad-driven conversion depends on player onboarding and deposits. Since 11 March 2026 (Presidential Decree No. 226 of 6 June 2025), a player account in a virtual gambling establishment can be opened only after identity verification (in person or by video), and one person may have only one account. In addition, banks set their own limits: for example, Belarusbank caps card-not-present payments, including payments to gambling merchants (MCC 7995), at BYN 600 per day for "Barkhat" cards and BYN 3,000 per day for other debit cards.

DE-4

Media, digital platforms & sports sponsorship. Brand awareness depends on access to television and radio (gambling ads are allowed only from 21:00 to 07:00), public transport, digital ad networks, and sports federations. In turn, Belarusian sports clubs and leagues have become financially dependent on betting and casino sponsorship.

DE-5

Enforcement against unlicensed competitors. The return on compliant, licensed advertising depends on the state blocking illegal offshore casinos and influencer or streamer promotions that bypass Belarusian tax and advertising rules.
