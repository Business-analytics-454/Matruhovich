Project 15

User Stories

US-1

As a player, I want to be able to disable push notifications and casino ad banners in my personal account so that my user experience is not intrusive.

US-2

As a marketer, I want to set up targeted advertising based on interests so that I don't show casino ads to those who are indifferent to them, thereby reducing the number of complaints.

US-3

As a casino owner, I want to implement a "smart" recommendation system (instead of aggressive banners) to increase brand trust and retain players.

US-4

As a regulator, I want every advertising creative to meet the requirements of Article 23 of Law No. 225-Z and carry an "18+" label and a risk warning, so that legal violations are avoided.
