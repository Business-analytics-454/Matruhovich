Assumptions

AS-1

The casino has an existing database of adult users and a lawful basis for processing their advertising preferences.

AS-2

Users have access to a personal account where they can adjust advertising frequency or opt out of advertising entirely.

AS-3

Existing advertising platforms support integration with the information system for managing advertising campaigns.

AS-4

The system has access to the data required to process advertising impressions and user settings, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

AS-5

The company has the technical resources and qualified specialists required to develop, implement, and maintain the system.

AS-6

The company can use alternative advertising channels, including informational content and email communications, for users who have provided the required consent.
