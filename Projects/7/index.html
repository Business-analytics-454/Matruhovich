Project 7

Major Features

FE-1

Frequency capping module. The system automatically hides a banner from a user once they have seen it 3 times in one session.

FE-2

Smart targeting. Displaying ads only to users who have previously shown interest in gambling or are subscribed to the newsletter (no mass, untargeted display).

FE-3

Personal account with privacy settings. The ability to unsubscribe from push notifications and email newsletters in one click.

FE-4

Analytics dashboard. Monitoring the "irritation" level (complaints, unsubscribes, banner blocks).

FE-5

Anti-spam integration. Verifying the quality of advertising materials (excluding shocking content).

FE-6

A/B testing system. Selecting the least intrusive ad formats (e.g., text blocks instead of pop-ups).

FE-7

"Responsible gaming" knowledge base. Useful content (articles on risks, limits) to attract a loyal audience.

FE-8

Geo-targeting module for outdoor advertising. Displaying ads only in certain districts where they will not disturb residents (e.g., not near schools).

FE-9

Access for marketers and ad moderators. Promptly responding to user complaints.
