Exclusions

What the system will deliberately not do.

EX-1

The system does not process deposits, payments, or withdrawals; these remain with the casino's payment providers and banks.

EX-2

The system does not verify player identity or age itself; it uses the verification status from the casino's existing identification process.

EX-3

The system does not create advertising creatives; it only checks, schedules, and limits their display.

EX-4

The system does not handle permits for outdoor billboards or their physical installation; it only plans geo-targeted placement.

EX-5

Sports sponsorship contracts and TV/radio media buying are outside the scope of the project.
