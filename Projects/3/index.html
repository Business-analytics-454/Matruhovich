Project 3

Solution

The creation of an information system for smart targeting and ad frequency management will automate the process of displaying advertisements, eliminating intrusive pop-ups and banners for users who show no interest. This will reduce negativity toward the brand, decrease the number of spam complaints, and redirect the budget toward less aggressive channels (content marketing, opt-in email subscriptions). As a result, advertising will become less irritating, and clients will receive only relevant offers.

For clients, this solution provides certain conveniences: they can adjust the frequency of ad displays or completely opt out of advertising in their personal account, which increases trust in the brand.
